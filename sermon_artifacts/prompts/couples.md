# Artifact — Couples' Conversation Guide

## What this artifact is

**Three questions** for a married couple to talk through together during the
week — over coffee, on a walk, at the end of a day. Drawn from the sermon's
theological frame, but tuned for the relational and spiritual life of a
husband and wife in covenant together.

## Constraints

- **Three questions exactly.**
- **Move through three different registers:**
  1. **Heart** — a question that surfaces what the sermon stirred or
     convicted in each spouse individually. "What did you hear?"
  2. **Marriage** — a question that presses how the sermon's truth applies
     specifically to *us as a couple* — patterns, blind spots, places we
     need to repent or rejoice together.
  3. **Prayer** — a question that opens us to pray for one another in
     light of what was preached. May surface a specific petition each
     spouse can carry for the other.
- **Presume a redemptive view of marriage.** Marriage is a gospel
  picture (Ephesians 5) and a sanctifying instrument. Questions should
  reflect mutual submission to Christ rather than role-policing.
- **No assumptions about role-distribution.** The questions should land
  the same whether the husband or wife asks them first. Avoid framing
  that requires one spouse to be the more spiritually mature.
- **Brevity matters.** Each question is one or at most two sentences. The
  couples will be tired; the prompt is the on-ramp, not the entire
  conversation.

## Output JSON schema

```json
{
  "title": "Short noun-phrase title (under 60 chars)",
  "questions": [
    "Heart question — what did the sermon stir individually",
    "Marriage question — how it shapes us together",
    "Prayer question — what we can pray for one another"
  ]
}
```

Output ONLY the JSON object. No markdown fences. No commentary.

## What to avoid

- Role-policing prompts ("Husband, are you leading well…")
- Marriage-counseling clinical language
- Questions that presume one spouse is the spiritual leader
- Generic "How are you feeling about…" prompts — keep them anchored to
  the sermon's specific theological move
- More than three questions — discipline matters; this is a doorway,
  not the whole house
