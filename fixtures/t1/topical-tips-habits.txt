Title: Five Habits of a Peaceful Week
Preacher: Fixture Preacher

Today I want to talk about five habits that will make your week more peaceful.
This is not a walk through one passage. I will borrow a line from Proverbs,
a line from James, a line from Philippians, and a line from the Psalms as
support for the topic.

Tip one: try to leave your phone in another room for the first hour. You should
do this every morning. Tip two: write three things you are grateful for. Here
are practical steps you can copy onto a card. Tip three: take a ten minute
walk. Tip four: how to reset after a hard meeting — count to ten and drink
water. Tip five: go to bed at the same time.

These are practical steps. I am not diagnosing a fallen condition or resolving
anything in the cross. I want you to have five keys you can use at work and in
your own prayer life. Your quiet time will improve if you follow the habits.
This is topical preaching: the theme of a peaceful week, with texts as
illustration, and mainly tips and imperatives for individual piety.
