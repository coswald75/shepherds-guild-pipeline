Title: Walking Romans 8
Preacher: Fixture Preacher

Open to Romans 8. We will take this passage verse by verse.

Look at verse 1. There is therefore now no condemnation for those who are in
Christ Jesus. The text says the court has already adjourned. Verse 2: the law
of the Spirit of life has set you free. Then he says the law could not do what
God has done. Look at verse 3. God sent his own Son in the likeness of sinful
flesh. Verse 4 follows: that the righteous requirement of the law might be
fulfilled in us. The next verse, verse 5, contrasts those who set their minds
on the flesh with those who set their minds on the Spirit.

Notice verse 6. To set the mind on the flesh is death. The Greek is phronema,
the mindset. Verse 7: the mind of the flesh is hostile to God. Verse 8: those
in the flesh cannot please God. We are walking the argument as Paul wrote it,
clause by clause, not collecting tips from around the Bible.

The fallen condition is hostility to God. The gospel resolution is already in
verse 3: God sent his own Son. This is consecutive exposition of one chapter,
not a topical survey.
