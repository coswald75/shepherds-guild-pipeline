Title: Hearing and Keeping the Word
Preacher: Fixture Preacher

The congregation has just heard Luke 11:27–36. This fixture is original test
copy for the T1 ingest path. It uses markdown headings the way Ligonier-style
transcripts mark movements, so the local chapterer can split without an API.

## A greater blessing than Mary

A woman in the crowd blessed the mother of Jesus. Our Lord did not deny her
honor. He redirected the blessing. Blessed rather are those who hear the word
of God and keep it. Hearing without keeping is a kind of unbelief. Keeping
without hearing is a kind of zeal without knowledge. The church is formed by
both: the ear that receives Scripture and the life that obeys it.

This is not a small pastoral aside. It is the hinge of the passage. Privilege
of proximity to Jesus is not the same as faith. Mary’s blessedness is real,
and still Jesus says there is a blessedness offered to every hearer who
receives the word and does it.

## An evil generation seeks a sign

Jesus calls his generation evil because it demands a sign after signs have
already been given. The demand sounds pious. It is often unbelief wearing a
lab coat. If God would only prove himself on my terms, then I would believe.
The Ninevites repented at the preaching of Jonah. The queen of the South
traveled to hear Solomon’s wisdom. A greater than Jonah and Solomon is here.

The sign of Jonah is not a parlor trick. It is death and resurrection. God
has already given the public, historical sign the church proclaims every
Sunday. To keep asking for a private miracle is to ignore the empty tomb.

## Light on a lampstand

No one lights a lamp and hides it. The eye is the lamp of the body. If the
eye is clear, the whole body is full of light. If the eye is dark, how great
is that darkness. Jesus is not giving an optometry lecture. He is talking
about perception of the kingdom. A generation can stand in daylight and still
walk in the dark if it refuses the word.

Take heed, then, that the light in you is not darkness. The T1 index only
needs this movement labeled so a later search for “sign of Jonah” or
“lampstand” can find the right span. Deep doctrinal loci wait for T2.
