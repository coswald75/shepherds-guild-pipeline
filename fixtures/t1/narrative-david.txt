Title: The Boy and the Giant
Preacher: Fixture Preacher

The story of David and Goliath is one we tell children, but I want us to live
in the narrative this morning. There was a man named Jesse, and his youngest
son kept sheep. Then he took five smooth stones from the brook. Then he stood
before the giant. Then he ran toward the line of battle. The parable of courage
is right there on the valley floor.

What lessons from this boy should we carry home? Be like David. We should
follow his example when the week feels oversized. David teaches us to have
courage this week. Let us imitate the faith of a teenager with a sling. The
example of this shepherd is the whole sermon. I am not tracing a redemptive
thread to a greater Son of David here — I am telling the story and drawing
the moral. Have courage like David at work and in your marriage.
